# Acme Widget Pro — FAQ

**Q: What is the battery life of the Acme Widget Pro?**
A: The Acme Widget Pro has a battery life of approximately 14 hours of
continuous use, or up to 3 weeks on standby.

**Q: Is the Acme Widget Pro waterproof?**
A: The Acme Widget Pro is rated IP67 — it can survive brief submersion in up
to 1 meter of water for 30 minutes, but it is not designed for swimming or
diving.

**Q: What is the warranty period?**
A: The Acme Widget Pro comes with a standard 1-year limited warranty
covering manufacturing defects. Accidental damage is not covered.

**Q: Does the Acme Widget Pro support wireless charging?**
A: Yes, the Acme Widget Pro supports Qi wireless charging at up to 10W.
