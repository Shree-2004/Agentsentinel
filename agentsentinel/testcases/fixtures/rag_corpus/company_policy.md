# Acme Corp Remote Work Policy

Acme Corp employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Remote work
requests must be approved by a direct manager at least 2 weeks in advance.

Employees on probation (first 90 days of employment) are not eligible for
remote work and must work on-site full time.

## Equipment

Acme Corp provides a laptop and a one-time $200 home-office stipend for
remote employees. Additional equipment requests go through the IT helpdesk
ticketing system.

## Core Hours

All employees, remote or on-site, must be reachable between 11:00 AM and
3:00 PM in their local time zone, referred to internally as "core hours."
